THE BASICS

What determines energy consumption?

Electrical energy consumption is commonly expressed in kilowatt-hours (kWh). An appliance with a higher power rating generally uses more energy when operated for the same amount of time.

For example, an appliance rated at 100 watts operating for 10 hours would use approximately 1 kilowatt-hour of electricity.
