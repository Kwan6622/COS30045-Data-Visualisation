About this data

A rate, not a headcount

Rates express the number of people held in prison per 100,000 of the general population. Sovereign nations and constituent administrations (such as Scotland and Northern Ireland) use circular markers, whereas overseas and autonomous territories use square markers and disaggregate from parent states.

Source: Institute for Crime & Justice Policy Research (ICPR), World Prison Brief.
