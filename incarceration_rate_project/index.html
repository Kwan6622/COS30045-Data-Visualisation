About this data

A rate, not a headcount

Rates express the number of people held in prison per 100,000 of the general population. The source dataset does not include total prison populations or historical observations, so those fields are intentionally not estimated here.

Source: Institute for Crime & Justice Policy Research, World Prison Brief.
