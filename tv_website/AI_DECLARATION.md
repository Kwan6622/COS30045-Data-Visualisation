# AI Declaration

Generative AI, primarily **ChatGPT (OpenAI)**, was used throughout the development and improvement of the Appliance Energy Consumption Website as a development, learning, and documentation assistant.

## 1. Purpose of Using Generative AI

My major is Artificial Intelligence, and although I have previous experience with web development, I had not worked extensively with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for some time.

Therefore, I used Generative AI to refresh my understanding of these technologies, assist with implementation, and improve the overall quality of the website and its data visualisations.

## 2. Initial Website Development (Version 1.0)

During the initial development of the website, Generative AI assisted with:

- Creating the basic structure of the Home, Televisions, and About Us pages.
- Implementing navigation, hover effects, and active page indicators.
- Developing responsive layouts using HTML and CSS.
- Implementing an interactive FAQ accordion using JavaScript.
- Organising the project into separate HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files.
- Explaining unfamiliar code and web development concepts.

The generated code was reviewed and adapted to meet the project requirements.

## 3. Website Improvements (Version 2.0)

For the updated version, Generative AI was used to support further development and improvements.

**Australian Electricity Pricing API**

ChatGPT assisted with understanding API integration, handling electricity pricing data, and implementing calculations that estimate annual electricity costs based on television energy consumption.

This helped improve the practical relevance of the website by connecting energy consumption data with Australian electricity pricing information.

**Data Visualisation Improvements**

Generative AI assisted with reviewing chart configurations, improving titles and labels, and organising additional visualisations to communicate the data story more effectively.

It also helped explain how different visualisation techniques can be used to compare television characteristics and energy consumption.

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Using KNIME**

ChatGPT was used to discuss and improve the KNIME workflows, including:

- Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Converting and grouping television screen sizes.
- Applying equal-size sampling to screen technology categories.
- Comparing televisions of similar screen sizes.
- Exploring the relationship between Energy Star Ratings and annual electricity consumption.
- Identifying limitations associated with sample imbalance and aggregated data.
- Improving workflow annotations and explanations.

These suggestions helped me better understand how data preparation and sampling methods can influence the interpretation of visualisations.

## 4. Verification and Personal Contribution

I reviewed and adapted AI-generated suggestions before incorporating them into the project.

My contributions included configuring and executing KNIME workflows, examining visualisation results, checking the relationships between television characteristics and energy consumption, and adjusting the website to communicate the findings more clearly.

I also reviewed how the website's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript components work together, including navigation, responsive design, interactive features, and electricity pricing integration.

The final selection of charts, interpretation of findings, and decisions about the presentation of the data story were made as part of my own development and learning process.

## 5. Limitations and Reflection

One limitation of Generative AI is that it can provide technically functional solutions that may not always be appropriate for a particular dataset or analytical objective.

For example, equal-size sampling can reduce differences in sample counts, but it does not automatically control for differences in television screen sizes. Similarly, comparisons involving Energy Star Ratings must distinguish energy efficiency from total annual electricity consumption.

These considerations demonstrated the importance of critically evaluating AI-generated suggestions rather than accepting them without verification.

Using Generative AI also helped me refresh my knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, API integration, and data visualisation principles.

## 6. Overall Declaration

Generative AI was used as a **development, analytical support, documentation, and learning tool**, rather than as a replacement for understanding the project.

AI-generated code, explanations, and recommendations were reviewed and adapted before inclusion in the final submission. I remain responsible for the final website implementation, data analysis, visualisations, and submitted documentation.
