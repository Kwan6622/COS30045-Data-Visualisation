TELEVISION BASICS

How much energy does a television use?

Television energy consumption can vary considerably depending on the display technology, screen size, brightness settings and amount of time the television is used.

01

Screen Size

Larger televisions can require more electricity because they typically have larger display areas and may require more power during operation.

02

Display Technology

Different display technologies can have different power requirements. Modern televisions may also include energy-saving features.

03

Usage Time

A television that is used for several hours every day will generally consume more electricity over time than one used occasionally.

SIMPLE CALCULATION

Understanding kWh

A simple way to estimate energy consumption is to multiply an appliance's power consumption by the number of hours it operates.

Energy (kWh) = Power (kW) × Time (hours)

For example, a hypothetical 100-watt television used for 5 hours would consume approximately 0.5 kWh during that period.

ENERGY TIPS

Ways to reduce television energy usage

01

Turn the television off

Turn the television off when it is not being used rather than leaving it running.

02

Use energy-saving settings

Many televisions provide settings that can reduce display brightness or power usage.

03

Consider usage habits

Reducing unnecessary viewing time can also reduce the electricity consumed by the television.
